The App is published as Ringtone Maker & Music Editor. For privacy questions or requests, contact: michealbright171189@gmail.com.

2. Summary

  • We do not require you to create an account to use the App.

2.1 Google Play Data Safety summary

  • Data collected by third parties: Advertising/analytics-related data may be collected by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), including identifiers and ad interaction information, subject to Google policies and your device settings.
  • Data you provide: Links (URLs) and files are processed when you actively use related features.
  • Data sharing: URLs you submit for link-based media features may be transmitted to third-party media-resolution services to provide requested functionality.
  • Security: Data transmission for network features is intended to use modern transport security (HTTPS) where supported by upstream services.
  • Deletion: You can delete locally exported files from your device at any time; uninstalling the app removes app-local data subject to Android behavior.

3.2 Information collected automatically

  • Advertising: Google’s advertising SDK may use identifiers (such as an advertising ID where permitted), device information, and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. See Google’s privacy documentation for details.
  • Network and diagnostics: Basic technical data may be processed when the App contacts servers (for example, when resolving a link or loading ads), including IP address and standard HTTP logs on third-party servers where applicable.

4. How we use information

  • To provide App features: editing, exporting ringtones, format conversion, mixing, playback, and related tools.
  • To fulfill link-based features: sending the URL you submit to a third-party API to retrieve playable or downloadable media, when you initiate that action.

  • To respond to your support or feedback messages.

6. Permissions (Android)

The App may request permissions needed for its features. Depending on your device and Android version, these can include:

  • Internet / network state: To load ads, analyze or download media from links, and use network-dependent features.
  • Storage / media access: To read and save audio, video, and exported files (including saving ringtones to your library where the system allows).
  • Modify system settings / ringtone-related capabilities: So you can set a saved sound as a default ringtone, notification, or alarm where the operating system permits and you grant the required system permission.
  • Modify audio settings: For playback and audio routing during preview or editing.
  • Phone state (where declared): May be used by advertising or media libraries for compatibility or ad delivery, depending on device and SDK behavior.
  • Advertising ID (where applicable): Used in connection with personalized or measurement features as allowed by Google and your device settings.

You can review or change permissions in your device Settings → Apps → Ringtone Maker & Music Editor → Permissions. Some features will not work without the permissions they require.
